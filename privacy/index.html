Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

This policy covers this website. It explains what information the site collects, why, and what happens to it.

What the contact and booking forms collect

When you use the contact form or the booking form, you enter your name, phone number, email address, the flight you are interested in, and anything you type in the details box. The booking form also records the date you picked and how many people are coming.

These forms do not store anything on a server. When you press the button, the site opens your own email app with a message addressed to fkfaoffice@gmail.com, filled in with what you typed. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

How the information is used

Florida Keys Flight Academy uses what you send to answer your request: to confirm availability, schedule a flight or lesson, and contact you by phone, text, or email about it. It is not sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not run analytics or advertising trackers and does not set its own cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and icons from Cloudflare's CDN, which receive standard request information (such as your IP address) when your browser loads them.

Third-party links

Some links go to other services, including Flight Schedule Pro for discovery flight booking, Google Maps for directions, and social media profiles. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a request to delete your information: call 844-KEYS-AIR or email fkfaoffice@gmail.com.
