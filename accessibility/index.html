Accessibility Statement

Last updated September 22, 2026

Florida Keys Flight Academy wants everyone to be able to find a flight, check a price and get in touch. This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What is in place

  • A skip link and a main landmark on every page, so keyboard and screen reader users can jump past the navigation.
  • Text and background colours checked to at least a 4.5 to 1 contrast ratio.
  • A visible focus outline on every link, button and form field.
  • Descriptive text for every meaningful image.
  • Form fields with labels, autocomplete hints and written error messages.
  • Menus, question lists and the booking window that work by keyboard, close with the Escape key, and report their state to screen readers.
  • Animation reduced for anyone whose device is set to reduce motion.

Known limitations

Some links lead to services the academy does not control, including Flight Schedule Pro for instant discovery flight booking, Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Keys Weekly and social media sites. Their accessibility is the responsibility of those providers.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, call 844-KEYS-AIR or email fkfaoffice@gmail.com. The academy will help you book by phone and fix the problem.
